This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Voicecast.

Text you type

Voicecast converts text to speech entirely inside your own browser, using your device's built-in speech engine. The text you enter is not sent to, stored on, or processed by any server operated by Voicecast.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party providers, such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your preferences, at Google's Ads Settings page.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Voicecast itself does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Analytics

Voicecast may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall visitor traffic, such as page views. This is used only to improve the site and is not linked to your identity.

Children's privacy

Voicecast is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
