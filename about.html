About Voicecast

Voicecast is a simple tool for turning written text into spoken audio, right inside your browser. Paste in a paragraph, an article, a script, or anything else you'd rather listen to than read, and it reads it back to you in a natural voice.

Why it exists

Reading long text on a screen isn't always the easiest way to take something in. Sometimes it's easier to listen — while cooking, commuting, resting your eyes, or just because listening suits you better. Voicecast exists to make that possible for any piece of text, with no setup required.

How it works

Voicecast uses your web browser's own built-in speech engine to generate audio on the spot. That means your text never leaves your device or gets sent to a server — it's processed locally by your browser, using whichever voices your operating system already has installed.

Who it's for

Students proofreading essays by ear, writers checking how their sentences sound out loud, people who find listening easier than reading, or anyone who just wants to hear a block of text instead of scanning it — Voicecast is built to be a quick, no-friction way to do that.

What's next

Voicecast is a small, ongoing project. Over time it may grow to support saving favorite voice settings, longer documents, and more listening controls. Suggestions are always welcome.
